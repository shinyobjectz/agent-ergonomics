## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS in site/, no build step, served by Cloudflare Pages at agentergonomics.org (the brief: "a quick
site").

## Users

People who build software with or for coding agents: engineers whose codebases agents change, and product people
whose tools, CLIs, docs and apps agents and humans both use (inferred from the skills' scope). They arrive from a
link (a post, a repo, a mention) and decide in under a minute whether the idea is worth installing.

## Product Purpose

agent-ergonomics is a free, MIT-licensed bundle of three agent skills: agent-ergonomics (untangle code and design
agent-facing surfaces), purpose-built (copy, flows and plans written for the reader), and first-read (understood on
first contact). The site states the philosophy behind them and sends people to the GitHub repo
(github.com/shinyobjectz/agent-ergonomics). Success: a visitor understands the one idea and installs the plugin.

## Positioning

One idea covers code, interfaces, copy and concepts: everything a reader needs is where they are, and nothing they
don't need is. The reader can be an agent changing code, an agent calling a tool, or a person using a product.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Generic: no product, company or customer is named on the site (brief).
- Install: `claude plugin marketplace add shinyobjectz/agent-ergonomics`, then
  `claude plugin install agent-ergonomics@agent-ergonomics`. Other agents copy the skills/ folders.
- No benchmarks, testimonials, user counts or customers exist. None may be shown.

## Brand Commitments

- The site must pass its own skills: no AI tells, no residue, one new idea at a time (brief).

## Evidence on Hand

README.md, NOTICE, and the three SKILL.md files with their references. No screenshots, logos or metrics.

## Product Principles

1. Everything a reader needs is where they are; nothing they don't need is.
2. Measure what readers do, not what they say.
3. Fewer special cases and fewer concepts beat better explanations of them.
